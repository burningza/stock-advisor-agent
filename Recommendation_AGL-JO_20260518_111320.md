## 🔍 REASONS FOR THE RECOMMENDATION

### 1. TRANSFORMATIONAL CATALYST — The Teck Resources Merger

The pending merger with Teck Resources (valued at ~R900 billion) is the single most compelling reason to own Anglo American. This deal will:

- **Create a top-5 global copper producer** with combined output of ~1.2 million tonnes per year
- **Shift the portfolio to >70% copper exposure**, making Anglo a **pure-play electrification & energy transition stock**
- **Unlock $500–800 million in annual synergies** (corporate overhead, procurement, technology integration)
- **Provide access to world-class growth projects** (Quebrada Blanca Phase 2, Highland Valley Copper, Resolution project in Arizona)

**The re-rating potential is substantial.** At a conservative 7.5x EV/EBITDA on combined 2027 estimated EBITDA of $12–14 billion (R200–235 billion), the implied equity value is **$70–85 billion** versus the current market cap of ~$48.5 billion — representing **45–75% upside**.

### 2. Successful Execution of Portfolio Simplification

The **coal sale to Dhilmar Ltd announced today (18 May 2026)** is a major de-risking event:

- **Up to R64.7 billion in cash proceeds** ($3.875 billion)
- **Removes all thermal and steelmaking coal exposure**, aligning with ESG mandates
- **Cash can reduce debt** (estimated net debt of R150–200 billion) or fund the Woodsmith project
- **Demonstrates management's ability to execute** complex divestitures

The exit from coal, platinum (Valterra demerger), and pending nickel sale shows a disciplined management team executing a clear strategy.

### 3. Copper: The Metal of the Megatrend

Copper prices are surging — **up 35.7% YoY to $6.25/lb ($13,780/tonne)** — driven by:

- **Structural supply deficit**: Mine production constrained by water shortages in Chile, falling ore grades, and labour disputes
- **Unrelenting demand growth**: EV adoption, grid expansion, data centres, renewable energy infrastructure
- **Critically low inventories**: LME and SHFE warehouse stocks near multi-year lows

Every **$0.10/lb change** in copper prices impacts combined Anglo Teck EBITDA by approximately **$200–300 million (R3.5–5 billion)**. At current prices, copper alone could generate **$8–10 billion in annual EBITDA**.
